# Income Category Definitions

> This file defines all income categories. Each category has a unique ID, name, emoji, and description.

## Category List

### 💰 Salary (工资)

**ID**: `salary` | **Emoji**: 💰

Employment income from full-time or part-time work.

**Keywords**: salary, wages, base pay, paycheck, 工资, 月薪, 薪资

---

### 🎉 Bonus (奖金)

**ID**: `bonus` | **Emoji**: 🎉

Performance-based income including:
- Year-end bonuses
- Performance bonuses
- Project bonuses
- Quarterly bonuses

**Keywords**: year-end bonus, bonus, performance, dividend, 年终奖, 奖金, 绩效

---

### 💻 Freelance (兼职)

**ID**: `freelance` | **Emoji**: 💻

Freelance and side job income.

**Keywords**: freelance, side job, gig, contract work, 兼职, 外快, 接单, 私活

---

### 📈 Investment (投资收益)

**ID**: `investment` | **Emoji**: 📈

Investment returns and financial income.

**Keywords**: investment interest, fund dividend, stock gain, dividend, 理财利息, 投资收益

---

### 🔄 Refund (退款)

**ID**: `refund` | **Emoji**: 🔄

Refunds and compensation.

**Keywords**: refund, return, compensation, 退款, 退货, 补偿

---

### 🎁 Gift (礼金)

**ID**: `gift` | **Emoji**: 🎁

Gift money and red envelopes.

**Keywords**: red envelope, gift money, 红包, 礼金

---

### ❓ Other (其他)

**ID**: `other` | **Emoji**: ❓

Other incidental income not matching above categories.

**Keywords**: other, incidental income, 其他, 偶然收入
