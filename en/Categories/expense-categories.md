# Expense Category Definitions

> This file defines all expense categories. Each category has a unique ID, name, emoji, and description.

## Category List

### 🍔 Food (餐饮)

**ID**: `food` | **Emoji**: 🍔

Food and dining expenses including:
- Restaurant meals (lunch, dinner, breakfast)
- Takeout and delivery
- Snacks and beverages
- groceries (if purchased separately from regular shopping)

**Keywords**: lunch, dinner, breakfast, takeout, restaurant, food, meal, snack, Shaxian, hotpot, BBQ

---

### 🚌 Transport (交通)

**ID**: `transport` | **Emoji**: 🚌

Transportation expenses including:
- Public transit (subway, bus)
- Taxi and ride-hailing (DiDi, Uber)
- Gas and parking
- Train and long-distance bus

**Keywords**: subway, bus, taxi, uber, parking, gas, train, DiDi, cycling

---

### 🛍️ Shopping (购物)

**ID**: `shopping` | **Emoji**: 🛍️

Shopping and consumer goods including:
- Online shopping (Taobao, JD, Pinduoduo)
- Clothes and shoes
- Electronics and gadgets
- Daily necessities and household items

**Keywords**: Taobao, JD, Pinduoduo, shopping, clothes, shoes, supermarket, electronics, phone

---

### 🎮 Entertainment (娱乐)

**ID**: `entertainment` | **Emoji**: 🎮

Entertainment and leisure including:
- Movies and TV shows
- Video games and Steam
- Music and streaming subscriptions (Netflix, iQIYI)
- KTV and social activities

**Keywords**: movie, game, music, video, Steam, Netflix, subscription, KTV

---

### 💊 Health (医疗)

**ID**: `health` | **Emoji**: 💊

Healthcare and medical expenses including:
- Hospital and clinic visits
- Pharmacy and medicine
- Health checkups and dental
- Medical treatments

**Keywords**: hospital, pharmacy, medicine, checkup, dental, clinic, medical

---

### 📚 Education (教育)

**ID**: `education` | **Emoji**: 📚

Education and learning including:
- Courses and tuition
- Books and learning materials
- Training and exams
- Subscriptions (edu platforms)

**Keywords**: course, tuition, book, training, exam, subscription, learning, tutorial

---

### 🏠 Housing (居住)

**ID**: `housing` | **Emoji**: 🏠

Housing and living expenses including:
- Rent and property fees
- Utilities (water, electricity, gas)
- Home repairs and furniture
- Housekeeping and cleaning

**Keywords**: rent, property, utilities, repair, furniture, renovation, cleaning

---

### 📱 Communication (通讯)

**ID**: `communication` | **Emoji**: 📱

Communication expenses including:
- Phone and mobile plan
- Broadband internet
- Data packages

**Keywords**: phone, mobile plan, broadband, data, communication

---

### 🎁 Gift (礼物)

**ID**: `gift` | **Emoji**: 🎁

Gifts and social expenses including:
- Red envelopes (红包)
- Gifts and gift giving
- Treating friends to meals
- Social obligations (份子钱)

**Keywords**: red envelope, gift, treat,人情, gift giving, 份子钱

---

### ✈️ Travel (旅游)

**ID**: `travel` | **Emoji**: ✈️

Travel and tourism including:
- Flights and hotels
- Travel and vacation
- Tickets and sightseeing
- Travel expenses

**Keywords**: flight, hotel, travel, ticket, vacation, sightseeing

---

### 💹 Investment (投资)

**ID**: `investment` | **Emoji**: 💹

Investment-related expenses including:
- Financial products (理财)
- Fund and stock investments
- Investment losses

**Keywords**:理财, fund, stock, investment, loss

---

### ❓ Other (其他)

**ID**: `other` | **Emoji**: ❓

Other expenses that don't fit the above categories.

**Keywords**: other, miscellaneous
